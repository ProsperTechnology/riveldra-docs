Know what changed.

Version history for Riveldra by Prosper Technologies.

0.9.3 Pre-release · 7 November 2026

Fixed native unbranded checkout-button contrast and unavailable-combination wording. Verified a 300-variant fixture, expanded editor settings and section lifecycle, and a no-charge simulated order in the app-free industrial demo.

Populated home, collection and product pages were audited three times on mobile and desktop. Average of six medians: performance 98, automated accessibility 100. A publisher iPhone Safari shopping and rotation check passed. These results do not replace the remaining browser, screen-reader, zoom and specialized commerce checks. No Theme Store approval is claimed.

0.9.2 Pre-release · 7 November 2026

First Shopify development-store QA fixes: removed stray characters from cart, contact, comment and recipient forms; hid empty SKU labels; connected the product color-scheme setting; and cancelled pending search suggestions when dismissed.

Verified live catalog navigation, stock filtering, sorting, variant selection, cart quantities/removal, saved notes, subscription pricing and checkout handoff. Private support delivery, screenshot attachments and automatic acknowledgements are now verified. Full device and accessibility testing, representative performance benchmarks and a finished demo remain release gates.

0.9.1 Pre-release · 7 November 2026

Adopted Riveldra as the theme name, replacing the previous ForgePro working name. Updated documentation, metadata links and package branding. Prosper Technologies remains the publisher.

Existing forgepro metafield keys and asset filenames are preserved for compatibility. No merchant data migration is required for this branding update. Live Shopify testing and the private merchant support workflow remain launch gates.

0.9.0 Pre-release · 7 November 2026

The first original local theme build, intended for Shopify test-store evaluation.

Added

  • Industrial visual system with paper, navy and gray color schemes.
  • Requested storefront page templates, including password and gift card.
  • Product option-value navigation, SKU/price/availability output and native purchase forms.
  • Native filters, sorting, predictive search and grid/list presentation.
  • Technical specifications, variant overrides, scoped equipment records and document links.
  • Configurable home-page and content sections, plus related/complementary recommendation output.

Validation so far

Nine local interaction checks passed and 13 adapted template fixtures rendered without duplicate IDs or unlabeled fields. A limited axe-core scan of eight local fixtures reported no violations; incomplete checks and contrast still require further review. These are local results, not live Shopify commerce tests or accessibility certification.

Before a commercial release

Complete Shopify installation/editor/commerce testing, representative performance measurement, browser and screen-reader checks, a real demo store, private support operations and final commercial review. Riveldra is the selected theme name; final trademark clearance is pending.

Updating a test installation

Duplicate your unpublished test theme before changing files. Preserve merchant settings, template configuration and data definitions. No prior commercial-installation migration is claimed for this candidate.
