RIVELDRA / CUSTOMIZATIONSHOPIFY THEME

A storefront that fits your business.

Use coordinated defaults, then adjust the details that matter to your customers.

Global theme settings

Colors
Three starting schemes: paper, navy and soft gray. Each pairs background/text and button/button-text colors. Check contrast whenever you change a pair.
Typography
Heading and body fonts use Shopify’s font pickers. The default is Assistant.
Layout and controls
Set page width from 1120–1600px and corner radius from 0–16px. Choose whether cards show vendors, SKUs and quick add. A setting also controls currency-code display.
Social media
Add optional LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube URLs. Empty entries produce no social links.

Header and footer

Header settings include a logo, main menu and sticky-header option. Search supports native results and predictive suggestions. Mobile navigation uses disclosure menus. Footer settings include business text, a menu, available localization selectors and Shopify payment icons.

Choose the right section

Section Use it for
Industrial hero A clear opening message, image or built-in bearing illustration, and one action.
Featured collections / products Departments and selected catalog ranges.
Featured product A real product with configurable purchase information.
Image with text Company expertise, application context and service information.
Content columns Capabilities, industries, technical expertise and service benefits.
Promotional banner A catalog document, contact link or quote-request callout.
Logo list / testimonials Authorized brand assets and genuine customer quotations.
FAQ / newsletter / video Useful answers, email signup and Shopify-hosted video.
Comparison table Merchant-authored comparison content; it is not a saved comparison app.
Custom Liquid A supported insertion point for your own Liquid.

Collection presentation

Choose grid or list as the default layout, 12–48 products per page, and filter/sort visibility. Customers can switch layouts without reloading. Filter changes use native query parameters, and active values can be removed individually.

Product information

Use product blocks for title, price, inventory, options, purchase controls, description and accordions. Main and featured product sections accept app blocks. Inventory counts are optional and use actual tracked inventory. The theme does not invent stock, urgency or delivery promises.

Mobile details

Sections stack, media scrolls horizontally, and wide compatibility tables have a labeled scrolling region. A sticky purchase link returns customers to the main options after the original form leaves view. There is one authoritative purchase state.
