Made for technical product information

ForgePro is an original Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme from Prosper Technologies, designed for industrial suppliers, hardware catalogs and replacement parts. The theme brings product identification, specifications, merchant-maintained compatibility and purchasing into a consistent storefront.

  • Manufacturer and SKU visibility on product cards.
  • Native catalog filtering, sorting and grid/list browsing.
  • Specification tables with optional variant-specific values.
  • Product-level compatibility records and document links.
  • Configurable sections and a responsive full cart page.
